# CosmoWhisper Native - User Guide & Tips

## 🚀 Pro Tip: Shift-to-Hold (Auto-Submit Override)

**The Problem:**
You have **Auto-Submit** enabled in your preferences because you like the speed of "Speak and Send". However, sometimes you want to dictate a longer, more complex thought that requires multiple recording segments.

**The Solution:**
CosmoWhisper now features a "Shift-to-Hold" override.

1.  **Hold the Shift key** on your keyboard.
2.  **Release your recording hotkey** (e.g., F9 or Mouse Button) while still holding Shift.
3.  **Result:** CosmoWhisper will insert your transcribed text into the active window, but it will **NOT** press Enter.
4.  You can then speak again, and the new text will be appended (with a smart leading space) to your previous thought.

## 🔧 Window Management
- **Always on Top:** Toggle this in the Dashboard Settings to keep the widget visible even when you switch to other apps.
- **Auto-Update:** The app checks for newer versions on startup and will prompt you to update.
