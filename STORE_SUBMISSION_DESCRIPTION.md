# Microsoft Store Submission - Product Description
## Status: Compliant with Policy 10.8.4

**Copy the text below for the 'Product Description' field in Partner Center:**

CosmoWhisper is the premium AI voice dictation suite for Windows, designed to let you type at the speed of thought. Powered by world-class transcription models and a sleek, transparent interface, it works in every application on your PC.

### Free Tier & Trial Functionality (Disclosure)
CosmoWhisper includes a permanent 'Starter' free tier.
- **Starter Plan:** Includes 60 minutes of high-accuracy AI transcription per month at no cost.
- **Minute Reset:** Your 60-minute quota resets automatically at the start of each monthly billing cycle.
- **Functionality disclosure:** The free tier includes access to all core dictation features. Advanced features such as unlimited transcription, race mode, and custom style context require a paid subscription.
- **No Expiration:** There is no time-limited trial period; you can remain on the Starter plan indefinitely.

### Key Features
- **Global Dictation:** Use voice-to-text in VS Code, Slack, Outlook, Notion, and more.
- **Privacy First:** Local fallback options and secure cloud processing ensure your voice data is yours.
- **Smart Commands:** Perform system actions or format text with voice-activated shortcuts.
- **Ultra-Fast Latency:** Results appear in sub-500ms for a seamless writing experience.

### Pricing Tiers
Detailed pricing for Pro, Ultimate, and Medical plans is available within the application and on our website. All subscriptions can be managed directly via your user dashboard.
